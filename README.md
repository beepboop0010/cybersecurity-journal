## Postępy
Day 1 — Cisco Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cisco — Introduction to Cybersecurity Course

In the first module, I learned:

What cybersecurity is protecting systems, networks, devices, and data from digital threats and unauthorized access.

How to protect personal data - using strong and unique passwords, enabling multi-factor authentication, keeping software updated, and being careful with suspicious messages and links.

How cybercriminals operate - using techniques such as phishing, malware, social engineering, and exploiting security vulnerabilities to gain access to systems or information.

What cyberwarfare is the use of cyber operations by states or state-sponsored groups to disrupt, damage, or gain access to another country's systems and infrastructure.
